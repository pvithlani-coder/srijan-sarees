Our story

Created from creation

Srijan means creation in Sanskrit. It is what happens every morning when a weaver in Varanasi sits at the loom. For generations, the weavers of Varanasi have produced some of the world's most treasured textiles. Srijan Sarees connects these craftspeople directly with buyers who genuinely value their work — no middlemen, no markups, no compromise on authenticity.

Privacy policy

Last updated: August 2026

Who we are

Srijan Sarees is a marketplace connecting handwoven saree buyers with weavers in Varanasi, India. We operate primarily through WhatsApp Business. Our website is srijansilk.com. Contact us at hello@srijansilk.com.

What information we collect

  • Your name and WhatsApp number when you message us
  • Your delivery address when you place an order
  • Payment confirmation details — we do not store card or UPI credentials
  • Photos and product details shared by weavers for listing purposes

How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To send order updates and tracking information via WhatsApp
  • To notify you of new arrivals if you have opted in
  • To resolve disputes or queries

Who we share your information with

We share your name and delivery address with the weaver fulfilling your order and with our shipping partner. We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

WhatsApp messaging

By messaging Srijan Sarees on WhatsApp, you consent to receiving order updates and relevant communications from us via WhatsApp. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP.

Data retention

We retain your order information for up to 2 years for accounting and dispute resolution purposes. You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing hello@srijansilk.com.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. Email us at hello@srijansilk.com for any such requests.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
